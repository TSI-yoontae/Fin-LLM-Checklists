Financial LLM Structural Validity Checklist

Self-Assessment Tool for Rigorous Financial Decision-Making Systems
Validity Score: 0% (0/0)
Strict Adherence Required: This framework sets minimum requirements for interpreting a backtest as evidence of deployment performance.
1. Temporal Sanitation Non-Anticipativity
Requirement Rationale (Bias Mitigation) Assessment & Comments
Parametric Knowledge Cutoff Disclosure
Are the latest dates of pre-training and fine-tuning disclosed? Does the evaluation window strictly follow these dates?
 Sin #1: Look-Ahead Bias
Prevents "Time Travel" where the model uses future knowledge embedded in its weights to predict the past.
Point-in-Time (PIT) Retrieval
Are retrieval sources constructed using ONLY documents available at the specific decision time? (No live web search or mutable wikis).
 Sin #1: Look-Ahead Bias
Prevents "External Leakage" where search engines rank historical docs based on future popularity or edits.
Full Trace Logging
Are prompts, retrieved documents, tool outputs, and timestamps logged for temporal auditing?
 Sin #1: Look-Ahead Bias
Ensures verifiability of the information set available at every specific decision step.
2. Dynamic Universe Construction Survivorship Control
Time-Indexed Tradable Universe
Does the simulation sample from the universe active at the decision time, including delisted or failed entities?
 Sin #2: Survivorship Bias
Ensures the model faces the true probability of failure inherent in financial markets.
Unbiased Task Sampling
Are benchmarks constructed without conditioning on ex-post news volume or present-day prominence?
 Sin #2: Survivorship Bias
Prevents testing only on "winners" that generated enough historical news to be remembered.
Failure Regime Reporting
Does the study report outcomes separately for surviving vs. non-surviving entities?
 Sin #2: Survivorship Bias
Prevents inflation of performance metrics by hiding losses from failed firms.
3. Epistemic Calibration Uncertainty & Abstention
Explicit Abstention Option
Does the action space include "No Trade" or "Do Not Know", and is it scored as a legitimate outcome?
 Sin #4: Objective Bias
Prevents the model from being forced to hallucinate or guess to satisfy a "helpful assistant" objective.
Calibrated Confidence/Distribution
Does the system output a predictive distribution or a structured uncertainty estimate?
 Sin #4: Objective Bias
Financial decisions require calibrated probability, not just fluent text (verbalized overconfidence).
4. Rationale Robustness Causal Validity
Evidence Grounding & Auditing
Are rationales explicitly grounded in retrieved documents? Are claims audited for factual hallucinations?
 Sin #3: Narrative Bias
Prevents the "Illusion of Understanding" where models suture disparate signals into false but coherent narratives.
Negative Controls & Entity Substitution
Does the system refrain from generating detailed causal stories when inputs are scrambled or entities are substituted?
 Sin #3: Narrative Bias
Tests if the "reasoning" is genuine or just a retrieval of memorized company-specific text patterns.
5. Realistic Implementation Cost & Latency
Latency-Aware Execution
Are trades executed at the price available AFTER generation is complete (accounting for slippage), not the price at the start?
 Sin #5: Cost Bias (Latency)
Zero-latency assumptions overestimate returns for time-sensitive strategies given the slow inference of LLMs.
Net Utility Reporting
Are metrics reported strictly after deducting transaction costs and operational inference costs?
 Sin #5: Cost Bias (Monetary)
Prevents complex, expensive pipelines from appearing superior to simple baselines when they are economically unviable.
